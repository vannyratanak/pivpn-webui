Manage the networks your VPN clients can reach.

Routes pushed to clients

Controls what destinations VPN clients send into the tunnel at all. Without a route here, a client never attempts to reach that network through the VPN — see the Firewall page's NAT/SNAT rules, which handle the return path once traffic arrives.

Push a new route

Applies immediately and restarts the OpenVPN service to take effect — already-connected clients will briefly reconnect.
